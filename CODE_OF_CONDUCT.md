# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in this project should be respectful and welcoming, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with empathy and respect.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and their effects on others.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, trolling, and publication of another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies to participation in this repository and any project spaces explicitly designated by its maintainer. Rajesh Amara, as the maintainer of this independent edition, handles moderation within those spaces. GitHub's upstream project maintainers are not responsible for enforcing this repository's policy.

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to address misconduct. The action taken depends on the behavior, its impact, and repeated violations; immediate restrictions may be appropriate for severe abuse. A review or response timeline is not guaranteed.

## Raising a concern

For a concern about public project content, an issue may contain a link to that content and a brief, non-sensitive request for maintainer review. Do not publish personal information, private communications, or sensitive allegations in a public report.

If private details are needed, request a private contact method first and wait for a suitable channel to be established. This repository does not publish a dedicated conduct-reporting address or promise a private reporting service. GitHub's reporting controls may also be used for abuse on its platform.
